Who We Are

From Francistown to
the Nation's Skyline

Kago Constructions was founded in Francistown by Baagi Kago — a civil engineer with a singular vision: to deliver world-class construction that reflects Botswana's ambition and identity. What started as a small residential contractor has grown into one of the country's most trusted Grade A construction companies.

Today, our portfolio spans residential estates, commercial towers, public infrastructure, and government projects across all nine districts of Botswana. We carry every project with the same pride and precision that defined our very first build.

Our Journey

Milestones That
Shaped Us

2008
Founded in Francistown
Baagi Kago establishes Kago Constructions with a team of 8, focusing on residential builds in the North East District.
2012
First Commercial Contract
Secured the Sefalana Retail Complex project — marking the company's expansion into commercial construction.
2016
Grade A Contractor Status
Achieved CIDB Grade A contractor classification, unlocking eligibility for government infrastructure contracts nationwide.
2019
Gaborone Office Opens
Opened our Gaborone CBD office to serve the capital's booming construction market. Team grows to over 120 staff and contractors.
2023
Masa Heights Handover
Completed Botswana's premier mixed-use commercial tower — a P42M flagship project delivered on time and certified ISO 9001.
